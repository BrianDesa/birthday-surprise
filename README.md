💌 A Birthday Website Made with Love

Turn your feelings into a personalized website and surprise someone special.

A romantic, interactive birthday website that lets you create a digital birthday surprise for your girlfriend, boyfriend, best friend, or anyone who holds a special place in your heart.

Instead of sending an ordinary birthday message, create a small digital experience filled with personal words, meaningful photographs, memories, animations, and interactive surprises. Customize the website with your own message and photos, publish it online, and send the link to your special person.

Some gifts are bought. Others are created with love. ❤️

✨ Features

This project combines a romantic visual design with interactive elements to make a birthday surprise feel personal and memorable.

💌 1. Interactive Envelope Opening

The experience begins with an envelope-style entrance that invites the recipient to open their surprise.
Creates a special introduction before revealing the birthday website.
Makes opening the website feel like receiving a personal letter.
Leads the recipient into the main birthday experience.

🎂 2. Personalized Birthday Greeting

Create a birthday greeting specifically for the person receiving the website.
Display their name and a customized greeting.
Add your own introduction and affectionate messages.
Make the first screen feel like it was created exclusively for them.

⏳ 3. Birthday Countdown Timer

A countdown timer adds anticipation to the birthday experience.
Displays the remaining time until the configured countdown target.
Creates a sense of excitement before the special moment.
Works alongside the birthday greeting and other interactive sections.
Tip: Check the countdown logic in the JavaScript before publishing. If the current implementation counts down to local midnight, make sure that behavior matches the birthday surprise you want to create.

📝 4. Personalized Love Letter

Write a heartfelt message that the recipient can read directly on the website.
Replace the original message with your own words.
Include inside jokes, shared experiences, gratitude, or wishes for the future.
Use the animated text presentation to make the letter feel more intimate.
There is no need to write a perfect literary masterpiece. A genuine message that sounds like you will usually mean more than a generic paragraph.

💗 5. Interactive Reasons Cards

Create a collection of cards explaining why the recipient is special to you.

Examples include:
Why I appreciate you
My favourite thing about you
A memory I will always treasure
Something you do that makes me smile
One thing I hope we experience together
Personalize each card with your own reasons. These little details can make the experience much more meaningful than a generic birthday greeting.

📸 6. Scattered Photo Memories Collage

Turn your favourite photographs into a digital memory wall.
The collage is designed to give the photographs a playful, scrapbook-inspired appearance.
Multiple photographs arranged in a scattered collage.
Slightly rotated images for a natural, handmade feel.
Thin white borders around the photographs.
Hover effects that bring a photograph forward.
Responsive layout for desktop and mobile screens.
Decorative sticker images for additional personality.
Replace the example photographs with your own pictures to tell your story through images.

🕯️ 7. Interactive Cake and Birthday Wish

The cake section adds a birthday-themed interaction to the experience.
A celebratory cake visual.
An interactive birthday-wish moment.
A playful way to make the website feel more like a birthday celebration.
You can use this section as the final surprise after the recipient has read the letter and explored the memories.

🌌 8. Animated Background

The website uses atmospheric animations to create a dreamy visual experience.
Animated stars and hearts.
A dark plum and wine-inspired background.
Rose and gold accents.
Elegant typography.
A romantic visual atmosphere without relying on a conventional birthday-card layout.

🐋 9. Holographic Whale Animation

One of the distinctive visual elements is the animated holographic whale scene.
The JavaScript draws stylized, three-dimensional-looking humpback whales using canvas rendering and animation.
Multiple whales with different sizes and movement patterns.
Smooth swimming and body-wave animation.
Turning and depth-like perspective effects.
Moving pectoral flippers and tail flukes.
Glowing edges and holographic colours.
Circuit-like details and travelling light effects.
The whales use a 2D canvas with a 3D-style projection rather than a full 3D rendering engine.
This creates a futuristic, dreamlike effect that makes the website feel more distinctive.

📱 10. Responsive Design

The layout is designed to adapt to different screen sizes.
Desktop and laptop screens.
Tablets.
Mobile phones.
Since the recipient may open the link on their phone, always preview the website on a mobile device before sharing it.
